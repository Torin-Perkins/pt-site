Fitness after 30, 40 and 50

Training at 35 isn't training at 20. And training at 55 isn't training at 35.

Your body, schedule and recovery change with every decade. Your program should too.

  • Joint-friendly programming. Smart exercise selection and swaps for cranky knees, shoulders and backs.
  • Muscle is medicine. Building and keeping lean muscle protects your metabolism, bones and energy as you age.
  • Built for a real schedule. Efficient sessions that fit around work, kids and travel.
  • Recovery counts. Sleep, stress and hormones are part of the plan, not an afterthought.
  • Sustainable nutrition. No crash diets. Real food, flexible targets and habits you can keep.

Meet your coach

I've been where you are.

I've battled obesity, an eating disorder and body dysmorphia. I gained 70 lbs, crash-dieted my way into passing out at the gym, and gained it all back. Fitness, done the right way, is what finally pulled me out, and it took me all the way to the bodybuilding stage.

As an ISSA-certified personal trainer, I take what I learned the hard way and turn it into a plan built for you: your goal, your body and your stage of life.
